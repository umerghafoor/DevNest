Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

DevNest is a local-first desktop application. This policy explains what data DevNest handles and what it does not.

Data we collect

None. DevNest does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data, and it contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or crash-reporting services. We do not operate servers that receive data from the app.

Data stored on your device

Settings, saved connections (such as SSH hosts), saved HTTP requests, and similar configuration are stored locally on your computer. This data never leaves your device through DevNest and is yours to view, back up, or delete at any time by removing the app's data directory.

Access to your system

To do its job, DevNest reads and acts on resources you point it at: local files, processes, ports, Docker, system services, terminals, and remote machines you connect to over SSH or Tailscale. This access is used only to show you that information and run actions you request. It is not sent to us or to any third party.

Network requests you initiate

DevNest connects to remote hosts, APIs, or services only when you instruct it to (for example, sending an HTTP request or opening an SSH session). Those connections are between your device and the destination you chose, and are subject to that destination's own privacy practices.

Website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies or analytics. The host may keep standard server logs under its own privacy policy. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when fonts are fetched.

Children

DevNest is a developer tool and is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
